After 18 years building software for other people — banks, sports tech, Amazon, brain research at EPFL, Swiss media, and an AI startup as CTO and co-founder — I'm building my own. One new learning & education app every month. This page is the scoreboard.

The rules

Every app starts with a written bet before the first commit: who it's for, which channel reaches them, what traction means as a number, and a kill-by date.

At the end of each month, the app is judged against its bet — not against how I feel about it. It either earns another month or gets killed with a public note. No exceptions for ideas I love.

About

I'm Dhanesh Neela Mana. I've built software since 2008: .NET banking systems at TCS, recruiting products used by 100,000 athletes at Front Rush, front-end at Amazon, semantic search for brain research at EPFL, and a legacy-platform rescue at Tamedia in Switzerland that I'm still proud of.
